CSC494 Dirt_Bike_Maintenance_Sensor_System This IoT system is designed to protect dirt bike engines during aggressive off-road riding. It continuously checks three critical conditions using sensors connected to an ESP32 microcontroller: oil level, coolant level (for liquid cooled models), and air box differential pressure (to detect clogged air filters from dust or mud). Real time data is transmitted to a mobile friendly dashboard or app, delivering notifications so riders can top off oil, refill coolant, or clean the filter before minor issues escalate into expensive damage like overheating or seizure.
Dirt bikes both 2 stroke and 4 stroke face extreme conditions: dust filled trails, sustained high RPMs, crashes, and frequent tipping. Neglecting basic maintenance is the leading cause of catastrophic engine failures, including low oil (causing lubrication failure, bearing seizure, and scored components), low coolant in liquid cooled bikes (leading to overheating, piston expansion/seizure, and warped heads), and clogged air filters (resulting in restricted airflow, rich running, power loss, and abrasive dirt ingestion that accelerates internal wear). These preventable issues account for the vast majority of engine "blow ups" in dirt bikes, as manual checks are often skipped mid ride or between sessions.
The system provides real time visibility of oil level (% full in sump/reservoir), coolant level (% in radiator/overflow bottle), and air box differential pressure (for filter restriction/clogging detection), plus threshold based alerts via push notifications (e.g., "Oil low top up soon"). Nonfunctional features include dust/water resistance, low power operation, and reliability in no Wi-Fi areas (local buffering with later sync). The tech stack features an ESP32 microcontroller, waterproof ultrasonic sensors (for oil/coolant levels), a differential pressure sensor (for the air box), a DC DC converter for stable power, and Arduino/C++ firmware.
