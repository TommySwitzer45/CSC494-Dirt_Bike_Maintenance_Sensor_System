CSC494-Dirt_Bike_Maintenance_Sensor_System IoT system designed to protect dirt bike engines during aggressive off-road riding. It continuously monitors three critical conditions using sensors connected to an ESP32 microcontroller: oil level, coolant level (for liquid-cooled models), and air-box pressure (to detect clogged air filters from dust/mud). Real-time data is sent via a mobile-friendly dashboard or app notifications — so you can top off oil, refill coolant, or clean the filter before small issues escalate into expensive engine damage like overheating or seizure.
Dirt bikes (both 2-stroke and 4-stroke) endure extreme conditions: dust-filled trails, high RPMs, crashes, and frequent tipping. Neglecting basic maintenance is the leading cause of catastrophic engine failures: Low oil (lubrication failure, bearing seizure, scored components), Low coolant (liquid-cooled bikescan have probelms of overheating, piston expansion/seizure, warped heads), Clogged air filter (restricted airflow, rich running, power loss, and abrasive dirt ingestion accelerating internal wear). These issues cause ~80-90% of preventable "blow-ups" in dirt bikes. Manual checks are often skipped mid-ride or between sessions.
The features of the device will show oil level (% full in sump/reservoir), coolant level (% in radiator/overflow bottle), air-box differential pressure (to detect filter restriction/clogging), and alerts (push notifications). Other features that are non-functional will be dust/water restant, low power, and reliable in no-WiFi areas. The tech stack will be ESP32 microcontroller, waterproof ultrasonic sensors (oil/coolant), differential pressure sensor (air box), DC-DC converter, Firware: Arduino/C++ on ESP32.
