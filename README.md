CSC494 — Dirt Bike Maintenance Sensor System
This IoT system is designed to protect dirt bike engines during aggressive off-road riding. It continuously monitors two critical conditions using sensors connected to a Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-C6 microcontroller: oil temperature and air box differential pressure. Real-time data is transmitted via Bluetooth Low Energy to a mobile-friendly dashboard or app, delivering notifications so riders know when their engine is at operating temperature, when it is running too hot, and when the air filter is becoming restricted before minor issues escalate into expensive damage like overheating or seizure.
Dirt bikes — both 2-stroke and 4-stroke — face extreme conditions: dust-filled trails, sustained high RPMs, crashes, and frequent tipping. Neglecting basic maintenance is the leading cause of catastrophic engine failures. Clogged air filters result in restricted airflow, rich running, power loss, and abrasive dirt ingestion that accelerates internal wear. Overheating from riding a cold engine too hard or pushing a hot engine beyond its limits leads to piston expansion, seizure, and scored components. These preventable issues account for the vast majority of engine failures in dirt bikes, as manual checks are often skipped mid-ride or between sessions.
The system provides real-time visibility of oil temperature via a DS18B20 probe mounted on the oil pan — alerting the rider when the engine is still cold and needs a warm-up period, when it is in the normal operating range, and when it is critically overheating. Air box differential pressure is measured using an MPXV7002DP sensor plumbed before and after the air filter, detecting restriction and clogging with a 0–100% clog severity reading and four status levels: Clean, Watch, Warning, and Critical. Threshold-based alerts are delivered via BLE push notifications such as "Air filter restricted - clean filter soon" or "Oil temperature high — ease off throttle."
The tech stack features a Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-C6 microcontroller, an Arduino Nano for DS18B20 temperature acquisition, an MPXV7002DP differential pressure sensor breakout for air filter monitoring, NimBLE for Bluetooth Low Energy transmission, and Arduino C++ firmware.
